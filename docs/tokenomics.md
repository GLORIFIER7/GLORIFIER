# GLORIFIER Tokenomics v1

## Supply

Maximum and initial supply: **1,000,000,000 GLR**.

| Allocation | GLR | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ecosystem | 400,000,000 | 40% |
| Treasury | 200,000,000 | 20% |
| Community | 150,000,000 | 15% |
| Liquidity | 100,000,000 | 10% |
| Contributors | 100,000,000 | 10% |
| Reserve | 50,000,000 | 5% |

Allocations are a planning model until independently approved and implemented. Contributor and treasury allocations should be subject to documented vesting/lockups before any public distribution.

## Utility

Potential utility includes authorized ecosystem access, service credits, marketplace mechanisms, and governance functions where legally and technically appropriate.

GLR does not represent guaranteed profit, guaranteed revenue, equity ownership, or a promise of appreciation.

## Governance boundary

GLORIFIER retains human final authority. No token contract function should permit automatic financial transfers, trading, or irreversible governance changes without an explicitly authorized mechanism.
